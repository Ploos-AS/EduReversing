# Final analysis report workflow

## 1. Scope and provenance

Identify samples/evidence, hashes, source of material, environment and safety constraints.

## 2. Executive findings

State only findings supported by the investigation. Attach confidence to each material conclusion.

## 3. Identification and program map

Document executable format, architecture, major functions/data regions and relevant relationships.

## 4. Recovered behavior

Describe input grammar, data flow, structures, transformations and observable outputs with evidence references.

## 5. Dynamic observations

Separate predicted behavior from observed runtime behavior. Record commands and inputs needed to reproduce observations.

## 6. Configuration and indicators

For each recovered value or proposed indicator, record provenance, scope, confidence and whether it is direct observation or interpretation.

## 7. Differential findings

When versions are involved, distinguish semantic, presentation, layout/toolchain and unresolved differences.

## 8. Misleading or non-behavioral artifacts

Record strings, dead code, metadata or decompiler renderings that could lead to an unsupported conclusion.

## 9. Alternative explanations

For important ambiguous evidence, preserve plausible competing interpretations and state what would discriminate between them.

## 10. Unknowns

Do not convert missing evidence into assumptions. List unresolved questions explicitly.

## 11. Reproducibility appendix

Provide exact tool versions, commands, hashes, transformations and controlled inputs sufficient for another analyst to reproduce the material findings.

## Quality gate

Before submission, every important claim should answer: **What evidence establishes this, how confident am I, and can another analyst reproduce it?**
