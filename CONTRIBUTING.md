# Contributing to EduReversing

EduReversing accepts educational material for defensive reverse engineering.

## Non-negotiable sample policy

Do not submit real malware or binaries of uncertain provenance.

Do not submit weaponized samples, credential stealers, ransomware, droppers, exploit payloads, persistence implants, bot clients, or captured malicious binaries, even when the intent is research.

Course fixtures must be:

1. purpose-built for education;
2. benign by design;
3. supplied as reviewable source code;
4. reproducibly built by the repository;
5. documented with the learning objective they demonstrate.

Generated binaries belong in ignored build directories and must not be committed.

## Preferred contribution form

For a new exercise, contribute:

- lesson text;
- benign fixture source;
- deterministic/reproducible build instructions;
- expected observations or instructor notes where appropriate;
- tests that demonstrate intended fixture behavior.

Keep offensive concepts bounded to what students need to recognize, analyze, contain, document, and defend against.
